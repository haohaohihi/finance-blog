# My Recommended Financial Tools

These are apps and platforms I've personally tested. Some of the links below are affiliate links, which means I earn a small commission at no cost to you if you sign up. I only recommend things I genuinely use and like.

## 🏦 Banking & Investing

- **[Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/)** — Commission-free stock and ETF trading. Great for beginners who want to start investing with small amounts. *Get a free stock when you sign up!*

- **[Acorns](https://www.acorns.com/)** — Automatically invests your spare change from everyday purchases. Perfect for people who want to invest but don't know where to start.

- **[Webull](https://www.webull.com/)** — Free stock trading with more advanced features than Robinhood. Good for when you're ready to level up.

## 💳 Credit & Debt

- **[Credit Karma](https://www.creditkarma.com/)** — Free credit score monitoring and personalized recommendations. I check mine weekly — it's completely free.

- **[Empower](https://www.empower.me/)** — Free budgeting tool that automatically tracks your spending and helps you find savings.

## 📊 Budgeting

- **The 50/30/20 Rule** — Not an app, but the simplest budgeting framework I've found. 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings/debt. Read my [full guide here](/posts/50-30-20-rule/).

## 📚 Books That Changed My Money Mindset

1. *The Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel
2. *Your Money or Your Life* by Vicki Robin
3. *The Simple Path to Wealth* by J.L. Collins
4. *I Will Teach You to Be Rich* by Ramit Sethi
